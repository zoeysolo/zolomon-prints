# wedding bouquet preservation in washington dc

Zolomon Prints is based just outside the District, in Silver Spring, which makes Washington DC the studio's home ground. If you were married at a hotel downtown, a museum or embassy, a rooftop, or one of the gardens around the city, your bouquet can become an archival scanography print without traveling far.

Because the studio is local, DC couples have options that out-of-town clients do not. Depending on timing, a bouquet can be handed off in person around the DMV rather than shipped, which means the flowers reach the scanner faster and at their freshest. The studio's scanography work was featured in Washingtonian in 2026.

## how it works in DC

Reach out before the wedding to hold a commission slot for your date. After the event, either arrange a local handoff or ship the bouquet to the studio. Fresh flowers scan best, so quick timing helps. Zoey scans the arrangement at 1200 DPI, composes the final image, and delivers an archival matte print in the size you choose.
