# wedding bouquet preservation in virginia

Virginia weddings often lean on the landscape: vineyards and manor houses in Loudoun County, gardens and historic estates across Northern Virginia, and venues out toward the Blue Ridge. The flowers are usually chosen to sit inside that setting, which makes them worth keeping. Zolomon Prints turns the bouquet into an archival scanography print, scanned at high resolution from the real arrangement.

The studio is just across the river in Silver Spring, so Northern Virginia is well within reach, and depending on timing a bouquet can be handed off locally rather than shipped. Zolomon Prints is a signed vendor partner with Rust Manor House in Leesburg and is on their recommended vendor list, so couples marrying there have a direct path to a print of their flowers.

## how it works in Virginia

Reach out before the wedding to hold a commission slot for your date. After the event, arrange a local handoff or ship the bouquet to the studio. Fresh flowers give the best color and detail. Zoey scans the arrangement at 1200 DPI, composes the final image, and delivers an archival matte print in the size you choose.
