# peonies

A peony is a big, generous flower, and that scale is what makes it striking on a scanner. A single open bloom can fill much of the frame, with ruffled petals crowding out from a full center. Scanned at high resolution, that abundance stays legible rather than turning into a blur, and the flower keeps the softness that makes people love it in the first place.

## how they scan

Peonies carry a lot of subtle color. A blush peony moves from near-white at the outer petals to a warm pink deep in the center, and scanography holds that transition cleanly. The petals are large and slightly cupped, so they catch light at different depths, which gives a scanned peony real dimension: some petals read sharp and forward, others recede into shadow.

Because the blooms are full and heavy, arrangement matters. A peony is often scanned as a single hero flower or a small cluster, so the structure has room to breathe and the center stays the focus.

## commissioning a print of your peonies

Peonies have a short season and open quickly, so they scan best fresh, right after the event. If peonies were part of your bouquet, reach out ahead of time to hold a commission slot, then ship the flowers after the day. The piece is scanned, composed, and printed on archival matte fine art paper in your chosen size, from small formats up to large wall pieces.
