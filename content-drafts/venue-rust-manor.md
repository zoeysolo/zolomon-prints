# bouquet preservation for rust manor house weddings

Zolomon Prints is a signed vendor partner with Rust Manor House in Leesburg, Virginia, and is on the venue's recommended vendor list. If you are marrying at Rust Manor, that means preserving your bouquet is already part of the network of vendors the venue trusts, and there is a direct path from your wedding day to an archival print of your flowers.

Rust Manor sits in Loudoun County wine country, and the flowers couples choose there tend to suit the setting: garden roses, ranunculus, seasonal blooms that look at home against a manor house and open grounds. Scanography keeps that arrangement exactly as it was on the day, scanned at high resolution and printed as fine art, rather than pressed flat or sealed in resin.

## how it works for Rust Manor couples

Reach out before your wedding to hold a commission slot for your date. Because the studio is nearby in Silver Spring, a bouquet from a Rust Manor wedding can often be handed off locally within a day or two rather than shipped, which keeps the flowers fresh for scanning. Zoey scans the arrangement at 1200 DPI, composes the piece, and delivers an archival matte print in the size you choose.

## for the venue

For Rust Manor, the partnership is simple: couples get a keepsake made from their own flowers, and the venue has a vetted vendor to point them to. Prints of the venue's own florals can also be arranged. If you run a venue and want to offer the same thing to your couples, this page is the model. Reach out to talk about a partnership.
