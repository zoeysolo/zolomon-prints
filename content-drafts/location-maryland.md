# wedding bouquet preservation in maryland

The studio is in Silver Spring, so Maryland is where Zolomon Prints started and where the service is easiest to use. Whether you married in Montgomery County, Baltimore, Annapolis, on the Eastern Shore, or in the western part of the state, your bouquet can become an archival scanography print close to home.

Being local matters for one practical reason: fresh flowers scan best. For many Maryland couples, the bouquet can be dropped off or handed over in person around the Silver Spring and greater DC area within a day or two of the wedding, which gets it onto the scanner while the color and structure are at their peak. If in-person handoff does not work, shipping is always an option.

## how it works in Maryland

Reach out before the wedding so a commission slot is held for your date. After the event, arrange a local handoff or ship the bouquet to the studio. Zoey scans the flowers at 1200 DPI, composes the piece, and delivers an archival matte fine art print in the size you choose, from small formats up to large wall pieces.

## for Maryland florists and venues

Maryland florists and venues are welcome to build scanography into what they offer couples. It is a keepsake that reflects the florist's own work, since the print is made from the exact arrangement they designed. The studio runs a referral program for local vendors. Reach out to talk it through.
