# wedding bouquet preservation for new york

New York weddings run on a tight, well-planned clock, and the bouquet is usually one of the last things anyone thinks about until it is wilting in a hotel room. Zolomon Prints preserves it as an archival scanography print, so the flowers you carried down the aisle become a piece of art rather than a memory that fades by the end of the week.

You do not need to be in the studio for this. Most New York commissions are handled by mail: the bouquet is shipped to the studio after the event and scanned there. That makes the service straightforward whether your wedding is in Manhattan or Brooklyn, upstate in the Hudson Valley, or out on Long Island.

## how it works from New York

Reach out before the wedding so a commission slot is held for your date. After the event, pack the bouquet and ship it overnight to the studio. Fresh flowers give the richest color and detail, so the sooner it ships, the better, though dried arrangements are also accepted. Zoey scans the flowers at 1200 DPI, composes the piece, and delivers an archival print in the size you choose, from small formats to large-scale wall art.

## working with New York planners and florists

If you are a planner or florist working with high-end New York couples, bouquet scanography is an add-on that outlasts the event and reflects well on the whole team. The studio works with planners on referral, and prints can be arranged as a client gift after the wedding. Get in touch to talk through how it fits your packages.
